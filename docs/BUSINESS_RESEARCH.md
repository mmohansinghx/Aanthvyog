# Business research

**Status:** Planned
**Research state:** no external market research or customer validation was conducted for this foundation task.

## Evidence discipline
Every future brief separates:
- **Verified fact:** supported by a dated, attributable source or observed study.
- **Estimate:** a calculation with disclosed inputs and uncertainty.
- **Assumption:** an unverified proposition to test.
- **Recommendation:** a proposed action based on evidence and tradeoffs.

Do not fabricate market size, competitor capabilities, customer quotes, conversion rates, willingness to pay, or operating costs. Capture source URLs, access dates, relevant notes, and limitations.

## Current evidence
- **Verified from founder brief:** AANTVYOG intends to build an AI Website & Web Application Builder and listed eight initial project categories.
- **Verified at initial workspace inspection:** no application, service configuration, market research, or customer evidence was present then. The later local UI preview is not market research or customer validation.
- **Assumptions:** target users may include small businesses, portfolio owners, education organizations, internal dashboard teams, and SaaS founders. Their priority, unmet needs, and willingness to pay are unknown.
- **Unknown:** competitor capabilities and pricing, demand by segment, alternatives used, acquisition channels, cost to serve, and differentiation.

## Research plan
1. Select one segment and interview representative users about current workflow, frequency, budget, failure cost, and alternatives.
2. Build a source-backed competitor matrix using official product pages, documentation, and pricing pages with access dates.
3. Map alternatives including agencies, freelancers, website builders, templates, low-code tools, and coding assistants.
4. Test prototype tasks and measure completion, quality, user effort, and trust in review/approval controls.
5. Estimate operating cost per successful project from current provider and infrastructure prices only after evaluation.
6. Record technical feasibility, security constraints, support burden, and migration costs.
7. Define experiments with hypothesis, sample, method, success threshold, owner, and stop condition before running them.

## Recommendation
Before selecting market position or pricing, validate one segment and one project category. This is a recommendation, not a claim that a particular segment is attractive.
