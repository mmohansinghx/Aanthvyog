# Company operating model

**Status:** In Design
**Owner:** Founder & Executive Office
**Scope:** Functional responsibilities; these departments are not 15 separate autonomous agents.

## Operating principles
The founder has final authority over vision, budget, paid services, major architecture, external integrations, production deployment, and company decisions. Department leads may recommend and execute within approved scope; they cannot bypass a founder gate. ChatGPT is a virtual strategy, research, and coordination partner. Codex is an engineering execution environment. Neither is represented as a human employee, legal officer, or registered company representative.

## Decision and escalation rules
1. A decision record identifies owner, options, evidence, risks, affected departments, approver, and date.
2. Recommendations are not approvals. A mandatory gate remains closed until founder approval is recorded.
3. Departments may not approve their own high-risk changes when independent review is required.
4. For unresolved security, privacy, cost, legal, or reliability risk, document and escalate instead of silently accepting it.
5. Use only the minimum set of agents needed. Departments are functions, not a mandate to create more agents.
