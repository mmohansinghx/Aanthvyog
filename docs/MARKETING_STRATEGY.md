# Marketing strategy

**Status:** Planned
**Market validation:** not yet conducted.

## Brand
- Name: AANTVYOG
- Tagline: Intelligence. United.
- Palette: #F5F2EA, #FFFFFF, #E8E8E5, #687C70, #B9B2A5, #343630.
- Direction: premium, minimal, clean, global technology brand.
- Avoid neon themes, excessive gradients, unnecessary animation, generic AI templates, and cluttered dashboards.

## Positioning hypothesis
AANTVYOG aims to help people move from a software idea to a reviewed, documented web project through a controlled workflow involving planning, implementation, and independent QA. This is a positioning hypothesis; do not market it as a live capability until implemented and evidenced.

## Audience
The initial audience is undecided. Candidate segments from the founder brief include business website owners, portfolio creators, education organizations, dashboard teams, SaaS builders, and CRUD application builders. Research must choose the first segment before committing to channel, pricing, or message.

## Claims policy
- Distinguish planned, in-design, in-development, implemented, tested, and released capabilities.
- Do not advertise unimplemented integrations, universal framework support, production readiness, security guarantees, or autonomous operation.
- Support quantified claims with reproducible evidence, date, scope, and limitations.
- Product and QA verify capability claims; founder approves major launch messaging.
- Do not imply AI replaces customer review or guarantees correct output.

## Initial experiments to consider
After choosing a segment: problem interviews, landing-page message tests, prototype usability, and a limited pilot with explicit scope and support. Define success thresholds before each experiment and do not present interest as purchase intent.
